## New string

**English:**

> --- KEYS AVAILABLE DURING EVERY LESSON ---  
> These function keys work at any time while a lesson is open:  
>   
>   Enter  —  Confirm you have tried the step and move to the next one.  
>   F1  —  Repeat the current instruction. Use this whenever you want NVDA to read the step again.  
>   F2  —  Hear a hint. If you are not sure what to do, press F2 for a helpful suggestion. Press it again for additional hints.  
>   F3  —  Skip the current step and move on. Use this if you cannot complete a step right now and want to continue.  
>   Escape  —  Stop the lesson. Press Escape three times to close NVDA Coach.  
>   
> --- KEYS AVAILABLE ANY TIME IN THIS WINDOW ---  
> These keys work whether a lesson is running or not:  
>   
>   F4  —  Open the NVDA Coach help documentation in your web browser. Press F4 once to arm it, then press F4 again within five seconds to open.  
>   F5  —  Send feedback to the developer by email. Press F5 once to arm it, then press F5 again within five seconds to open.  
>   F6  —  Toggle lesson sounds on or off. Press once to silence sounds, press again to restore them.  
>   F7  —  Open your profile. Set your name, your instructor's name, and your training center. This information personalizes your lessons and appears on your certificate of completion.  
>   
> --- LESSON NAVIGATION ---  
>   Ctrl+N  —  Move to the next lesson in the current chapter.  
>   Ctrl+B  —  Go back to the previous lesson.  
>   Ctrl+R  —  Restart the current lesson from the beginning.  
>   
>
