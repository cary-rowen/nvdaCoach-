# NVDA Coach 1.6.0 — what the reviews found and we did not fix

Two adversarial passes ran over the built 1.6.0 package on 2026-09-20: one
breaking the code, one walking the course as a beginner. Between them they
raised 44 findings. The ones that were fixed are in `CHANGELOG.md`. These are
the ones that were not, kept here so they are not lost.

Nothing below was run inside NVDA. NVDA was not installed on the machine that
did the review, and neither was Word or Excel. Everything here comes from
reading the add-on's code and NVDA's own source, so treat every claim as
needing one live confirmation before somebody acts on it.

---

## Worth doing next, in rough order

### Timers cannot be cancelled, and one of them speaks after you stop

`lessonRunner.py` has a `_cancelPendingTimer()` that can never cancel
anything: no `wx.CallLater` result is ever assigned to `self._pendingTimer`.
The only assignments are `= None`. There are 13 `wx.CallLater` sites across
`__init__.py` and `lessonRunner.py`.

Two consequences worth caring about:

- `startLesson` schedules the lesson introduction 600 ms out with no
  `isActive` guard. Press Escape inside that window and NVDA tells you the
  lesson stopped and then announces that it is starting.
- `terminate()` destroys the window while callbacks are still pending, so a
  reload of plugins or a disable within about three seconds of finishing a
  lesson fires a callback on a destroyed wx object.

### Escape stops the lesson, and five lessons tell you to press Escape

Chapter 1 Lesson 2 — the second command in the whole course — ends "press
Escape to close it and come back here". If `NVDA+N` did not work, which is
the single most common beginner failure and which that step's own hint
acknowledges, Escape lands in the Coach window and stops the lesson instead.
The same trap is set in Chapter 1 Lesson 12, Chapter 4 Lesson 8, Chapter 6
Lesson 3, and Chapter 3 Lesson 8.

`beginEscapeSequence` also sets a counter without speaking, so a student
pressing Escape repeatedly closes NVDA Coach entirely and is never told that
is what is happening.

Suggested: during an active lesson, make the first Escape ask rather than
act.

### Enter means two things at once in the browse mode chapter

Every gesture step appends "press Enter or click Next Step" — while the
student is standing in a web browser, where Enter follows the link they are
on. `doc/en/practice.html` has eight external links. Lessons 2 through 8 all
end this way; only Lessons 9 and 10 tell the student to press `NVDA+Shift+C`
first.

Suggested: make the appended cue chapter-aware.

### The manual still says NVDA+Shift+C works in any other window

Three places in `doc/en/readme.html` say "Return to the Coach window from any
other window". With the Excel hand-off in place that is no longer true.
Chapter 3 Lesson 8 also sends the student into Word or Excel and then relies
on the reflex the whole course has trained.

Suggested: teach Alt+Tab as the way back in that lesson, and correct the
three readme lines. Alt+Tab is already taught in Chapter 1.

### The synth settings ring can leave NVDA speaking a language you do not read

Chapter 6 Lesson 2 teaches the increase and decrease keys with no restriction
on which ring position you are on. Land on Voice by miscounting, press
increase, and eSpeak switches to one of its 40-odd languages. The recovery
(`NVDA+Ctrl+R`) is not mentioned until three steps later.

Suggested: move the recovery to before the first adjustment is taught, and
name the specific danger.

### Six hints in four languages still teach a Reset to defaults button

Correction 3 in `translators/FACTUAL-CORRECTIONS-1.6.0.md` was applied to
English only. Spanish, Polish and both Chinese still tell the student to
press a button that does not exist in the Speech category. A sighted user
shrugs; a blind student Tabs the whole panel, hears no such button, and
concludes they are Tabbing wrong.

### The Spanish manual advertises lessons Spanish does not have

`doc/es/readme.html` still says "4 nuevas lecciones (45 en total)" and names
two of them by chapter and lesson number. Spanish ships 41. A Spanish learner
navigates to Capítulo 3, Lección 8 and it is not there.

The same shape applies to Polish, Russian and Turkish. Either restore the
four lessons in those languages or strip the references and the count.

### The practice browser is only closed in English

`__init__.py` matches the window title against the literal string
"NVDA Coach Practice Page". All eight other languages translate that title,
so the practice browser is never closed at the end of the browse mode
chapter. In English it matches on substring against a top-level window title,
so it can close a browser window with eleven other tabs in it, or a Notepad
document with that phrase in its filename.

### Two more crash paths

- Pressing `Ctrl+N` twice inside one second desynchronises the lesson index:
  the second press schedules a lesson that is then dropped silently, leaving
  the stored index one ahead of the lesson actually playing.
- Escape during the two second pre-roll of a browse mode lesson still opens
  the practice page afterwards.

### A practice page that fails to open says nothing

`os.startfile` is wrapped in a try that only writes to the log. The student
is told to press H for headings on a page that never opened.

## Smaller, and cheap

- **Profile-scoped settings.** `nvdaCoach` is not in NVDA's
  `BASE_ONLY_SECTIONS`, so the student's name, instructor, training centre
  and the sounds toggle are stored per configuration profile. Set your name
  while an app-triggered profile is active and it is gone when that profile
  deactivates — and the certificate says "Student".
- **Downloads is assumed to be `~/Downloads`.** OneDrive Known Folder Move
  redirects it routinely, and the error message then blames the folder being
  inaccessible when it is simply somewhere else.
- **A hint tells the student to open WordPad**, which Windows 11 has not
  shipped since 24H2. That hint is the fallback for students who do not own
  Word, which is most people learning at home.
- **Alt+Tab is used in Lesson 7 and taught in Lesson 14.** Lessons 7, 8 and 9
  all depend on it first.
- **Shift-to-pause is taught as a certainty.** NVDA's guide says "if pausing
  is supported by the current synthesizer". The Coach drops the caveat and
  then builds a worked example on it.
- **The manual lists Chapter 1 in a different order from the picker.**
  Battery Status is 14th in the manual and 5th in the product, so every
  lesson from 5 to 13 is off by one.
- **Three language aliases are dead.** `zh_MO`, bare `zh` and bare `pt` are
  not locales NVDA ever reports, because `getLanguage()` only returns a
  locale NVDA itself has a catalogue for. `zh_HK` and `pt_PT` do work and
  were confirmed. Harmless, but the comment beside them promises something
  that cannot happen.
- **The alias is consulted before the base language.** The day somebody
  contributes `lessons/pt/`, a `pt_PT` reader will still be sent to `pt_BR`.
- **About 456 KB of `.po` files ship in the package.** NVDA reads only `.mo`.
  `doc/ru/readme.md` is a stray source file, present for Russian only.
- **`build.bat` has no exclusions** and relies on a comment telling the
  operator to delete `__pycache__` by hand. It never calls
  `tools/verify_package.py`, which would catch it.
- **Turkish has a much thinner store listing and manual** than every other
  language — 344 bytes against 1694 to 3225. Worth asking Umut whether that
  is deliberate.
- **Two blocks of dead code:** `CoachWindow.showDrillProgress` has no callers
  and builds an unwrapped English string; `_cancelPendingTimer` can never
  cancel anything.
- **Space silently advances a step** and there is no previous-step command,
  so a hand resting on the space bar loses your place.

## One thing both reviews singled out as worth protecting

Chapter 1 Lesson 1, *Understanding Command Categories* — teaching the three
layers (Windows, application, screen reader) before teaching a single
keystroke, and framing NVDA commands as "take no action, only inform". It
gives a beginner a model for why the same key behaves differently in
different places, which is the thing that actually defeats people in month
one, and the payoff is picked up again in Chapter 4 Lesson 10.

Close second: Chapter 1 Lesson 6 step 3, which tells the student the truth
about the tool's own limits — Control and Shift cannot be practised inside a
lesson because they would interfere with it — and gives them a real-world
drill instead. Admitting what the tool cannot do, to a user who cannot see
it, is the right instinct.
